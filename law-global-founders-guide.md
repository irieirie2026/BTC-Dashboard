# The Law — Global Founders: Where to Build & Where to Live

**Audience:** Italian and other **EU residents** who are founders of crypto / blockchain / Web3 startups and want options **outside Italy and the EU** for company seat, tax residence, banking, and lifestyle — while understanding what EU/Italian rules still attach to them.

**Priority deep dives:** Panama · Guatemala · Japan · Cuba  

**Also covered:** UAE, Singapore, Switzerland (non-EU), UK, US (selected states), El Salvador, Paraguay, Georgia, Portugal/non-habitual residual notes (exit), Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Mexico, Costa Rica, Argentina, Uruguay, Kazakhstan, and comparison lanes for “digital nomad” vs “real move.”

> **Not legal, tax, immigration, or financial advice.** Rules change. Relocation and company formation create multi-jurisdiction exposure (Italy exit, CFC, PE, CRS, substance, sanctions). Verify with Italian *commercialista* / international tax counsel, immigration counsel in the destination, and local corporate counsel **before** you move, incorporate, or open banks. This is an educational map for founders, not a “flag-of-convenience” playbook.

## How to use this section (clever structure)

Think in **four layers**. Mixing them up is how founders get double-taxed or stranded without a bank.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  1. YOUR TAX RESIDENCE (where YOU are taxed as a person)    │
│     Italy today → exit tests, days, centre of vital interests │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  2. COMPANY SEAT (where the LEGAL ENTITY is formed)         │
│     HoldCo / OpCo / IP / CASP entity — may be different     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  3. BANKING & PAYMENTS (where money actually moves)         │
│     Local bank + EMI + crypto CASP rails                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  4. LIFESTYLE OPS (where you sleep, hire, CoL, visas)       │
│     Visa path, healthcare, safety, internet, schools        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Typical Italian crypto founder mistake:** open a Panama SA, keep living 200+ days in Milan, keep Italian banks, market to EU users without MiCA — and assume “offshore = free.” **Personal tax residence + MiCA perimeter + banking KYC** still bind.

**Useful combinations (illustrative only):**

| Goal | Layer 1 (you) | Layer 2 (co) | Layer 3 (bank) | Layer 4 (life) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stay in IT, EU product | Italy | IT SRL or EU CASP home | IT bank + EU CASP | Italy |
| Relocate LatAm, keep global product | Panama / PY / SV residence path | Panama SA / Estonian / UAE | Multi-currency EMI + local | Panama City / Asunción |
| Asia base, product company | Japan / SG / TH long-stay | JP KK or SG Pte | Local + international | Tokyo / SG / Chiang Mai |
| Extreme constraint / research | *High caution* | Often blocked | Often blocked | Cuba — see deep dive |

Use **The Law → Overview** map for **Bitcoin legal status** by country. Use **EU MiCA / Founders** for **EU client-facing** products. Use **this guide** for **where to put the company and yourself** outside the EU bubble.

## Leaving Italy / the EU as a person (expat fundamentals)

### Tax residence exit (Italy) — concepts only

Italian personal tax is generally **worldwide income** while you are **tax resident**. Exit is a **facts-and-circumstances** analysis (civil registration, 183-day style tests, centre of vital interests, family, home, economic ties). **Unregistering from anagrafe** without a real move is a classic audit magnet.

**Founder checklist before “I’m not Italian tax resident anymore”:**

1. Written memo from Italian international tax counsel (not a Telegram group).
2. Actual days calendar (Schengen + third countries).
3. Housing, family, and Italian company director roles documented.
4. **Exit tax / capital gains** on substantial shareholdings or crypto portfolios where applicable — fact-specific.
5. **Quadro RW / monitoring** history clean; open foreign accounts reported while resident.
6. New country **tax ID / residence certificate** plan.
7. Double tax treaty map (Italy ↔ destination), if any.
8. Social security / healthcare continuity (EHIC ends usefulness outside EU).

### EU citizenship is not “EU tax residence”

Keeping an Italian passport while living in Panama does **not** by itself keep you Italian-tax-resident — but **days in Italy**, Italian home, and Italian companies can. Conversely, moving does not erase past Italian filing years.

### Corporate vs personal

An Italian founder can own a **foreign company** while remaining Italian tax resident — then Italy taxes you on **salary, dividends, CFC rules, PE risk** of foreign cos managed from Italy. **Substance** and **where decisions are taken** matter.

### Immigration vs tax vs company

| Instrument | What it solves | What it does not solve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist visa / 90 days | Short scouting | Tax residence, banking |
| Digital nomad visa | Legal stay for remote work | Always tax residence (often not) |
| Temporary residence / work | Longer stay | Automatic low tax |
| Permanent residence / citizenship | Stability | Instant bank credibility |
| Company incorporation alone | Legal entity | Your personal tax or visa |

## Priority deep dive: Panama

### Snapshot for crypto founders

| Dimension | Summary (educational, verify live) |
| --- | --- |
| **Why founders look here** | Territorial tax tradition, USD economy, logistics hub, Latin America HQ narrative, relative ease of company formation |
| **BTC legal status** | Generally **legal to hold/trade** (see Overview map: Panama) — not legal tender; comprehensive crypto statute has been **politically contested** — re-check Superintendencia / MEF |
| **Company forms** | **Sociedad Anónima (SA)** classic; SRL options; foundations for asset holding (specialist advice) |
| **Personal tax theme** | Often marketed as **territorial** (foreign-source income themes) — **anti-abuse, local-source, and substance** rules matter; do not DIY |
| **Banking** | Possible but **KYC-heavy**; multi-bank + EMI stack common; crypto founders need clean narrative |
| **Language** | Spanish daily; English in finance/legal hubs |
| **Time zone** | Convenient to US East / LatAm |

### Cost of living (order-of-magnitude, USD / month, mid-2026 style bands)

| Lifestyle | Panama City | Interior / beach towns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean solo (rent room/studio, local food) | ~1,200–2,000 | ~800–1,400 |
| Comfortable solo / couple | ~2,500–4,500 | ~1,500–2,800 |
| Family + international school | ~4,500–8,000+ | lower housing, schools drive cost |

**Major cost drivers:** Casco / Punta Pacifica rents, international schools, private health insurance, imported goods. Fiber internet in city is generally strong for remote teams.

### Visas & residence (expat paths — verify with immigration counsel)

- Tourist entry for many EU passports (duration depends on nationality/stamps).
- **Friendly Nations / professional / pensionado / investment** style residence categories have historically been used by Europeans — **requirements change**; use a licensed *tramitador* + lawyer.
- Path to permanent residence / citizenship is multi-year and not automatic via company alone.

### Company setup (practical)

1. Local lawyer + resident agent.  
2. SA incorporation, bylaws, shares, directors (nominee rules are supervised — substance expected for banking).  
3. Notice of operations / municipal where required.  
4. Bank account (hardest step) — prepare source-of-funds, Italian exit story, crypto policy.  
5. Accounting & annual fees.

**Rough professional costs (ballpark):** incorporation **USD 1.5k–5k+**; annual maintenance **USD 1k–3k+**; banking introductions extra.

### Crypto / banking / ops notes

- Prefer **authorised partners** for any EU-facing CASP services (MiCA still applies to EU clients).
- Panama entity + EU product ≠ MiCA free pass.
- Good for **HoldCo / LatAm commercial** stories with real travel and local footprint.

### Who Panama fits

- Founders building **LatAm + USD** businesses.  
- Those ready for **Spanish** admin and serious banking packs.  
- Less ideal if you need **Japanese-style** infrastructure or **EU retail** as core market without EU entity.

## Priority deep dive: Guatemala

### Snapshot for crypto founders

| Dimension | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Why founders look here** | Low cost of living, Central America base, Spanish, growing remote-worker interest |
| **BTC legal status** | Generally **legal to hold**; formal VASP framework **thinner** than Brazil/Chile (see Overview: Guatemala — confidence often medium/low) |
| **Company forms** | SA / SRL-style commercial companies via local counsel |
| **Personal tax theme** | Territorial / local-source themes appear in regional planning — **verify** with GT + IT counsel; do not assume zero tax |
| **Banking** | Conservative; crypto narrative harder than Panama |
| **Safety / ops** | Security varies sharply by area — lifestyle due diligence is non-negotiable |

### Cost of living (USD / month, approximate)

| Lifestyle | Guatemala City | Antigua / lake towns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean solo | ~700–1,300 | ~600–1,200 |
| Comfortable couple | ~1,500–2,800 | ~1,200–2,400 |
| Family + private school | ~2,500–5,000+ | similar / school-driven |

**Strengths:** food, domestic help, housing value vs Western Europe. **Watch:** private security choices, healthcare quality variance, air quality in city, earthquake preparedness.

### Visas & residence

- Tourist stays for EU passports (check current stamp rules).  
- Temporary residence via work, investment, rentista-style categories — **use immigration counsel**.  
- Not a classic “instant digital nomad visa” destination compared to some peers; structure carefully.

### Company + crypto practicality

- Cheaper setup than Panama/US, but **less mature crypto banking**.  
- Better as **low-CoL ops base** or regional commercial entity than as global exchange HQ.  
- Pair with **EMI / foreign bank** only where compliant — banks may reject pure crypto.

### Who Guatemala fits

- Cost-sensitive founders wanting **Central America life**.  
- Spanish-speaking teams.  
- Not ideal as sole “prestige” jurisdiction for institutional investors.

## Priority deep dive: Japan

### Snapshot for crypto founders

| Dimension | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Why founders look here** | Huge market, high trust, advanced crypto exchange regulation (JVCEA / FSA), safety, infrastructure |
| **BTC legal status** | **Legal / regulated** — exchanges registered; crypto generally treated as property for tax (see Overview: Japan) |
| **Company forms** | **KK** (Kabushiki Kaisha), **GK** (Godo Kaisha) — GK often leaner for startups |
| **Personal tax** | Progressive national + inhabitants tax; crypto gains often **miscellaneous income** style (high marginal rates possible) — specialist *zeirishi* required |
| **Banking** | Excellent once resident; **non-resident / foreign founder** onboarding is slow and document-heavy |
| **Language** | Japanese required for deep ops; English in tech pockets (Tokyo) |

### Cost of living (USD / month, approximate)

| Lifestyle | Central Tokyo | Regional cities (Fukuoka, Osaka suburbs, etc.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean solo | ~1,800–2,800 | ~1,200–2,000 |
| Comfortable couple | ~3,500–6,000 | ~2,200–4,000 |
| Family + international school | ~6,000–12,000+ | lower housing, schools still costly |

**Strengths:** safety, trains, healthcare, payments UX. **Costs:** rent in 23 wards, international schools, dining out can add up.

### Visas & residence (high-level)

- **Business Manager**, **Engineer/Specialist in Humanities**, **Highly Skilled Professional**, startup programmes in selected cities — each has capital / office / employee tests.  
- EU citizens still need a **status of residence**; visa runs are not “digital nomad free-for-all.”  
- Path to PR is multi-year with points systems for some categories.

### Company setup

1. Office address (virtual offices scrutinised for Business Manager).  
2. GK/KK registration via judicial scrivener.  
3. Tax numbers, social insurance when hiring.  
4. Bank account — often needs representative’s residence card.  

**Ballpark:** incorporation professional fees **¥200k–¥800k+** depending on structure; capital requirements depend on visa path more than company law minimums.

### Crypto / product notes

- Selling exchange/custody into Japan requires **local registration** — do not passport EU MiCA into Japan.  
- Building **non-custodial software** from Japan is different from operating a CASP.  
- Corporate crypto treasury tax and accounting are specialist topics.

### Who Japan fits

- Founders targeting **Japanese users** or deep Asia credibility.  
- Those OK with **high tax if resident** unless structure is carefully planned with counsel.  
- Not a “low tax relocation” story for most people living full-time in Tokyo.

## Priority deep dive: Cuba

### Snapshot — high constraint, high caution

| Dimension | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Why someone might look** | Lifestyle curiosity, cultural projects, regional research — **not** a standard crypto startup HQ |
| **BTC legal status** | **Restricted / tightly controlled** (see Overview: Cuba); banking isolation + sanctions dominate |
| **Company formation for foreigners** | Highly restricted; joint ventures / specific investment vehicles under state frameworks — **not** a simple SA for EU founders |
| **Banking** | Severe limits; international cards and transfers often fail |
| **US / EU sanctions & compliance** | **Critical.** Italian/EU founders and any US-touching stack must run **sanctions counsel**. Many banks and CASPs will refuse Cuba-related activity. |
| **Internet / ops** | Improving but not a default remote-work hub for global SaaS |

### Cost of living

- Dual economy dynamics (CUP / USD / MLC history) make simple “USD/month” tables misleading.  
- Tourist-facing costs in Havana can feel mid-range; reliability of power, internet, and inputs is the real cost.

### Visas

- Tourist cards / visas for Europeans — tourism ≠ right to run a global crypto company.  
- Long-term residence and entrepreneurship for foreigners is **not** comparable to Panama/UAE.

### Founder recommendation (educational)

Treat Cuba as **out of scope** for standard “incorporate + bank + raise” playbooks unless you have specialist counsel for **investment law + sanctions + banking**. Prefer Panama/Mexico/Spain-language hubs for LatAm, or EU/Japan/SG for regulated product.

## Company formation patterns (from an Italian/EU resident founder)

### Pattern A — Stay tax-resident in Italy, foreign OpCo

- IT taxes you personally; foreign co may be **CFC** / **PE** if managed from Italy.  
- Use when product needs foreign entity but **you still live in Milan**.  
- High documentation burden.

### Pattern B — Real move + foreign tax residence + local company

- Aligns Layer 1–4.  
- Requires immigration + tax exit done properly.  
- Best long-term coherence.

### Pattern C — Move personally, keep EU CASP for EU clients

- Personal life in Panama/Dubai; **EU CASP** serves EU users under MiCA.  
- Common for compliant global products.  
- Substance in CASP home state still required.

### Pattern D — Multi-entity stack

```
HoldCo (e.g. SG / UAE / PA)
    ├── IP / protocol co
    ├── EU CASP (DE/NL/MT/…) for EU clients
    └── US sub (if US GTM)
```

Only with **transfer pricing**, intercompany agreements, and real people in each box.

## Decision trees

### “I live in Italy today — what should I optimise first?”

```
Do you sell crypto services to EU clients?
├─ YES → Read EU MiCA / Founders first; pick EU CASP home state
│         Relocation is Layer 1–4 on top, not instead of MiCA
└─ NO (software, non-EU users, research)
   └─ Do you want lower personal tax via move?
      ├─ YES → Pick residence country first (Panama/UAE/PY/…)
      │         then company + bank
      └─ NO → Keep IT residence; foreign co only with CFC/PE memo
```

### “Panama or Guatemala?”

```
Need credible LatAm HQ + banking path?
├─ YES → Panama first
└─ NO — pure CoL + Spanish life?
   └─ Guatemala / Mexico / Paraguay comparison
```

### “Japan or Singapore for Asia?”

```
Selling to Japanese consumers / needing JP trust?
├─ YES → Japan entity + visa path
└─ NO — regional HQ English + funds?
   └─ Singapore (or Dubai as dual Asia-Europe hub)
```

## Expat life checklist (any country)

1. **Visa** path that matches work (founder activity ≠ tourist).  
2. **Tax residence** certificate / rules; Italy exit memo.  
3. **Health insurance** from day one.  
4. **Banking**: personal + corporate; backup EMI.  
5. **Housing** contract suitable for residence applications.  
6. **Phone / 2FA** — do not strand OTP on Italian SIM without roaming plan.  
7. **Accounting** in local language.  
8. **Power of attorney** in Italy for residual admin.  
9. **Estate / wills** if multi-country assets.  
10. **Schooling** if family (often the real bottleneck).  
11. **Security** routine (especially GT / some LatAm cities).  
12. **CRS** reporting: banks will report — assume transparency.

## Closing disclaimer & sources to verify

**Not advice.** Immigration categories, tax rates, and banking policies change. Cuba and any sanctioned or high-risk jurisdiction require **specialist sanctions counsel**. Japanese and Singapore crypto business lines require **local licences**. Panama/Guatemala corporate and tax claims online are often oversimplified.

**Verify with:**

- Destination immigration statutes and licensed attorneys  
- Local tax authority guidance + Italy Agenzia delle Entrate / commercialista for exit  
- Central banks / securities regulators (crypto)  
- Numbeo / official stats / local expat reports for CoL (triangulate)  
- Your bank’s policy before you move funds  

**Suggested founder workflow**

1. Write a one-page goal: market (EU/US/LatAm/Asia) + lifestyle + risk tolerance.  
2. Shortlist **2 residence** + **2 company** options max.  
3. Italy exit memo + destination residence memo in parallel.  
4. Banking pre-intro before you give up Italian accounts.  
5. Re-check **Overview** legal status + **MiCA** if any EU user remains.  
6. Revisit quarterly — especially tax budgets and visa programmes.
