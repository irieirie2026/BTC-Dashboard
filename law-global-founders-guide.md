| --- | --- |
| 1. Tax residence | Where are **you** taxed as a person? |
| 2. Company seat | Where is the **entity** formed? |

Do not assume a foreign company alone changes Italian personal tax while you still live in Italy.

---

## Leaving the EU as a person (short)

- Worldwide taxation while EU/Italian tax resident is the default planning assumption.  
- Exit is **facts-based** (days, centre of vital interests, home, family, economic ties).  
- Coordinate **exit-year** capital gains (including crypto) with the **first year of new residence**.  
- Keep **SOF/SOW** files for banks: exchange exports, wallet history, Italy tax returns.
